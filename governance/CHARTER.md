# Foundry Charter

Status: **Canonical**  
Adopted: 2026-10-02

## Mission

UIGS-Foundry turns repeated project experience into reusable, inspectable, machine-readable engineering knowledge. It reduces rediscovery, repeated failure, contradictory guidance, stale reports, and dependence on conversational memory.

## Constitutional principles

1. Research success and failure before designing.
2. Real execution paths outrank simulations when they determine the final result.
3. Evidence levels must remain distinct; detection, inference, estimate, probe, real sample, device validation, and final output are not interchangeable.
4. Unknown is a valid state.
5. Move errors earlier when doing so is cheaper and safer.
6. Preserve reversibility until validation is complete.
7. Long-lived state has one authoritative owner.
8. Invalidate only what depends on changed state.
9. Reports, diagnostics, failure logs, test matrices, reproducible tests, and release rules are first-class engineering outputs.
10. Projects remain separate execution systems; share proven knowledge rather than creating an accidental monolith.
11. Capture broadly; promote conservatively.
12. Provenance is mandatory for reusable claims.
13. Platform implementations may differ while preserving semantic intent.
14. Foundry must not become a second copy of product source code.
15. Automation must surface uncertainty, staleness, and failed writes.

This Charter governs Foundry and cross-project knowledge promotion. Detailed product implementation belongs to lower layers and product repositories.
