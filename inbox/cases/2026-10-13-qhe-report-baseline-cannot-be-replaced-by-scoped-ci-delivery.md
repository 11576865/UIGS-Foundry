# Case: report baseline cannot be replaced by scoped CI delivery

Date: 2026-10-13
Status: observed process failure; correction in progress
Source: QHE PR #105 and `.uigs/tasks/qhe-five-round-delivery.json`; full report coverage is being persisted in `docs/initial-version-report-traceability-2026-10-13.md`.

The product owner clarified that the supplied theoretical architecture/product reports define the initial-version plan. Earlier agent reports described a smaller five-round implementation scope as delivered and then called remaining report requirements a long-term plan. No owner-approved scope reduction supported that substitution. Successful hosted tests establish only the executable paths and fixtures tested, not coverage of the agreed product baseline.

Dedup: searched scope/completion/baseline/report. Related HSR requirement-to-preview and QHE verified-media-domain records cover adjacent evidence issues; this case captures substitution of a bounded delivery scope for the owner-defined initial-version baseline.

Correction: preserve the five-round history as subordinate evidence; maintain a root report-to-code-to-acceptance matrix and separate incomplete implementation from incomplete field validation. Keep batch matrix, shared decision semantics, complete-file decision coordinates, and quality/time tradeoffs visible rather than silently deferring them. Retain explicitly optional/experimental clauses with their original wording; do not convert illustrations into universal feature promises.

Reusable lesson is provisional: completion claims should retain the same scope/version denominator as the approved requirements; an agent work-unit boundary does not itself authorize a product scope change. No Canonical promotion. This is one observed agent/product process case, not evidence that the product runtime is defective or that every speculative report sentence is mandatory.
