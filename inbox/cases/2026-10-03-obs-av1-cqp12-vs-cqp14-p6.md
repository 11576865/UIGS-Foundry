# Case: OBS NVENC AV1 CQP 12 vs CQP 14 at identical P6 settings

Date: 2026-10-03
Status: Case
Scope: OBS recording / NVENC AV1 / load comparison

## Source evidence

Two consecutive completed OBS recordings used the same:
- 3840x2160, 60 FPS output;
- NVENC AV1;
- preset P6;
- HQ tuning;
- quarter-resolution multipass;
- 4 B-frames, B-ref mode 2;
- look-ahead 8 frames;
- AQ enabled.

Only the reported CQP differed:
- recording A: CQP 12;
- recording B: CQP 14.

Recording A ended with 10 lagged rendering frames (0.1%) and 10 skipped encoding frames (0.1%).
Recording B also ended with 10 lagged rendering frames (0.1%) and 10 skipped encoding frames (0.1%).

The second recording also took much longer to acquire the game-capture hook after recording start than the first, showing that source initialization timing can vary independently of encoder settings.

## Interpretation

This single paired observation does not show a meaningful performance-load difference between CQP 12 and CQP 14 at otherwise identical NVENC settings. It supports treating CQP primarily as a quantization/bitrate-quality control rather than as the first suspect for encode-compute overload.

The sample is not controlled enough to claim general equivalence; scene complexity, bitrate, storage throughput and GPU state were not measured.

## Reusable implication

When comparing two OBS/NVENC profiles, hold preset, multipass, look-ahead, B-frame settings, resolution and FPS fixed. Do not infer encoder-load differences from CQP alone; compare OBS rendering/encoding lag counters, output bitrate/file size and storage throughput.

Do not promote to Canonical from this single case.
